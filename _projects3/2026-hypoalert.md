On February 21-22, 2026, I participated in the annual 24-hour BigRed//Make-a-thon hosted by the Cornell Maker Club with a team made up of a few of my close friends, competing under the theme, "Wearable Technologies." We designed a minimum viable product (MVP) for a noninvasive wearable device that can help detect signs of hypoglycemia. Our final presentation and demonstration video can be seen below.
